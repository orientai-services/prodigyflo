## Permission justification (paste verbatim, tweak names as needed)
> SCS Integration is a first-party app used solely by our own business, Solar Contract Services, to retrieve
> the leads generated by our own Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads. When a person submits one of our lead forms,
> Meta sends a `leadgen` webhook to our endpoint; we use `leads_retrieval` to fetch that lead's submitted contact
> details (name, email, phone) and form answers, and import them into our own CRM so our sales team can follow up
> on the person's request. We only ever access leads for our own Page — never any other page or business. The data
> is stored securely and used only to contact the person about the solar services they inquired about, consistent
> with our privacy policy at https://prodigyflo.ai/privacy.

## Data Use Checkup answers
- **What data:** the person's name, email, phone number, and their answers to our lead form.
- **Purpose:** contact the person about their solar inquiry; sales follow-up; keeping a record of the conversation.
- **Sharing:** not sold; shared only with our own service providers (CRM, communications, hosting).
- **Retention:** kept while needed to serve the inquiry and for legal purposes, then deleted/anonymized.
- **Deletion path:** users email `privacy@prodigyflo.ai` (documented in the privacy policy) and we delete on request.

## Reviewer instructions (paste into "How to test")
1. Our integration receives Meta Lead Ads webhooks at `https://prodigyflo.ai/api/meta/leads`.
2. When someone submits our page's lead form, Meta delivers a signed `leadgen` notification.
3. We verify `X-Hub-Signature-256` with our app secret, then call
   `GET /{leadgen_id}?fields=id,created_time,field_data` with our System-User token (`leads_retrieval`).
4. We import the returned fields into our CRM as a new client record for sales follow-up.
5. To reproduce: in the **Lead Ads Testing Tool**, pick the *Solar Contract Services* page + form and click
   **Create lead** — it appears in our CRM within seconds.
